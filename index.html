PeakCut

Short clips from long videos and live streams, posted to your own channels.

What it does

PeakCut is a clipping tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It watches videos and live streams, finds the best moments, cuts them into vertical short clips with captions, and can post those clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses YouTube

When the owner presses Connect, PeakCut asks Google for permission to upload videos to the channel they choose and to read basic channel details (its name and the view counts of videos PeakCut posted). It never deletes videos, never changes other channel settings, and never acts on any channel that was not connected by its owner.

Who it is for

PeakCut is a personal tool used by its owner. It is not a public service and does not have other users' accounts.
