Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short

PeakCut runs on your own computer. Your videos, clips and Google sign-in stay on that computer. Nothing is sent to the people who make PeakCut, and nothing is sold or shared.

What PeakCut uses from your Google account

When you connect a YouTube channel, PeakCut asks Google for:

  • Uploading videos (youtube.upload): to post the clips you choose, or that you set to post automatically, to that channel.
  • Reading your channel (youtube.readonly): to show the channel's name and the view counts of clips PeakCut posted.

PeakCut does not delete videos, change channel settings, read private messages, or use any other Google service.

Where your data is kept

The sign-in Google gives PeakCut (an access token) is saved only in PeakCut's data folder on your computer. Clips, titles and the record of what was posted are kept there too. PeakCut has no server of its own that receives your data.

Who your data is shared with

Only with Google, and only to do what you asked: clips and their titles, descriptions and hashtags are sent to YouTube when they are posted. If you set up an AI service in PeakCut, the words spoken in a video are sent to that service to find good moments and write titles; that is the service you chose, under its own terms. Your Google account data is never sent to an AI service.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PeakCut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In PeakCut, press Remove next to the channel in Settings, Connected apps. Its saved sign-in is deleted.
  • Or remove PeakCut at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Its access ends at once.
  • Deleting PeakCut's data folder deletes everything it kept.

Children

PeakCut is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
